#PlayMyJam
## ✨ Comprehensive Feature Set

### ⚡ Real-Time Event-Driven Queue
At the heart of Play My Jam is a highly responsive, event-driven queue system powered by technologies like Supabase Realtime. When a user requests a song, upvotes a track, or when a DJ approves a request, the state is instantaneously broadcasted to all connected clients. This eliminates the need for manual page refreshes and provides a seamless, dynamic user experience.

### 🔍 Extensive API Integrations (Spotify & YouTube)
To ensure users can always find exactly what they want to hear, the platform is deeply integrated with the Spotify and YouTube Data APIs. This provides access to millions of tracks, high-quality album art, and accurate metadata, preventing the ambiguity of vague song descriptions.

### 🗳️ Democratic Voting Mechanism
To prevent a single user from dominating the playlist, the platform employs a democratic upvoting system. Once a song is requested, it enters a public queue. Other guests can upvote the track, naturally pushing crowd favorites to the top of the DJ's dashboard. This ensures the music reflects the collective mood of the venue.

### 💎 VIP Experience: Pay for Priority (<<extend>>)
Understanding the commercial needs of venues, Play My Jam includes a premium monetization layer. Guests who do not want to wait for their track to naturally climb the queue can opt to securely pay for a "Priority Request". This bypasses the standard voting mechanism and highlights the request directly on the DJ's screen, offering a VIP experience while generating additional revenue for the venue.

### 📍 Geofencing & Venue Verification
To maintain the integrity of the venue's queue and prevent spam from outside users, strict verification protocols are in place. Users must either physically verify their location (via GPS) or enter a unique, time-sensitive "Venue Code" displayed on the venue's screens to interact with the active queue.
